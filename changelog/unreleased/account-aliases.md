---
title: Service tiers for additional provider accounts
type: change
authors:
  - colinperel
created: 2026-11-22T00:00:00Z
---

Additional accounts registered under another provider name, such as pi-multi-pass's `anthropic-2` and `openai-codex-2`, now use the service tier of the provider whose API and official endpoint they share. `/fast` and `/ultrafast` work on these accounts, and the account shares one setting with its provider.
